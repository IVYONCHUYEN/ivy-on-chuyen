#speakers: Host=Charon, Hoa=Leda
Host: Hoa, you moved from the city back to your home village. Why?
Hoa: People assume it was because of the pollution, and that was part of it. But the main reason was my parents – they're getting older, and I wanted to be near them.
Host: Was it hard to find work?
Hoa: I was worried about that, but I work online as a graphic designer, so I can live anywhere with a good internet connection.
Host: How has the village changed?
Hoa: Honestly, it's quieter than I remember. Many young people have left for the city, and the school has fewer pupils every year. That makes me a bit sad.
Host: What would encourage young people to stay?
Hoa: Better internet and some small businesses. I'm actually planning to open a co-working space here next year, so others can do what I do.
