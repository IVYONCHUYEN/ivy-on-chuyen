Good morning, everyone, and welcome to the Summer Study Skills course. My name is Laura and I've been teaching here for eight years. This course is mainly designed for students who are preparing for important entrance exams. Over the next four weeks, you'll learn how to plan your time and how to take notes effectively. Many students write down every word the teacher says, but that's not very useful. Instead, we'll show you how to use key words and simple diagrams. Classes take place every Tuesday and Thursday in the main hall, starting at half past two. Each week you'll get one written assignment, and the deadline is always Monday morning. Please don't skip any sessions, because each one builds on the last. If you miss a class, you'll need to ask your group leader for the handouts. Finally, the course fee includes all materials, but not the end-of-course trip to the science museum.
