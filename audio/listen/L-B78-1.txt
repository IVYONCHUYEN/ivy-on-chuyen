Good evening. Tonight's report looks at how our city has changed. Thirty years ago, fewer than two million people lived here; today the population is over eight million. Most newcomers arrive from the countryside looking for work in factories and offices. This growth has brought problems. The biggest complaint from residents is traffic congestion: the average commuter now spends ninety minutes a day on the road. Housing is another issue, as rents have doubled in the last decade. To tackle these problems, the city has opened its first metro line, and plans to build three more by 2035. The council is also creating satellite towns outside the centre, where land is cheaper. Not everyone is convinced, however. Some experts argue that unless jobs move to these towns too, people will simply commute even further.
