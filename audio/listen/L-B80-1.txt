In today's lecture, I'd like to look at one of the biggest demographic changes of our time: population ageing. In Viet Nam, the number of people over sixty-five is growing faster than almost anywhere else in Asia. This is the result of two trends: people are living longer, and families are having fewer children. Some economists describe this as a 'silver tsunami'. The challenges are clear. A smaller workforce must support a larger number of retired people, which puts pressure on the pension system and on healthcare. However, there are also opportunities. Older people today are often healthy and active, and many want to keep working part-time. Companies that offer flexible hours can benefit from their experience. Meanwhile, a whole new market is emerging for products and services designed for older customers, from easy-to-use smartphones to travel packages.
