#speakers: Host=Charon, Hana=Leda
Host: My guest today is Hana, whose travel blog has over a million readers. Hana, how did it all start?
Hana: Honestly, by accident. I was working in a bank and I hated it. My sister suggested I write about my weekend trips, mostly to keep myself sane. I never expected anyone to read it.
Host: Your most popular post is about Ha Giang. Why do you think it was so successful?
Hana: People assume it's the photos, and the scenery there is breathtaking. But readers kept telling me it was the honesty. I wrote about getting lost, being cold, and how the homestay family had to rescue me. People liked that it wasn't perfect.
Host: You've been quite critical of some famous destinations.
Hana: Yes. Some places are, frankly, overrated. I visited one beach that appears on every list, and it was so crowded you couldn't see the sand. That was a real let-down.
Host: What about eco-tourism? It's very fashionable.
Hana: I'll be honest – I was sceptical at first. A lot of companies just put the word 'eco' on their brochures. But I joined a tour in the Mekong Delta run by local farmers, and it completely changed my mind. The money stayed in the community, and we learned so much.
Host: Finally, what's next for you?
Hana: I'd love to write a book, but my real dream is to travel through Viet Nam by bicycle, from north to south. Maybe next year!
