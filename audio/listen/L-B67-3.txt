#speakers: Host=Puck, Minh=Charon
Host: Minh, you're only nineteen and already a successful water puppeteer. How did you start?
Minh: My grandfather performed in our village. At first I wasn't interested – I wanted to be a footballer – but when he got ill, I started helping him, and I fell in love with it.
Host: What's the hardest part?
Minh: People think it's controlling the puppets, but that comes with practice. Actually, the hardest part is standing in cold water for hours in winter.
Host: Do young audiences still enjoy water puppetry?
Minh: They do, especially when we add modern stories. Last year we performed a show about plastic pollution, and children loved it.
Host: What are your plans?
Minh: I'd love to take our show abroad. My dream is to perform in Japan.
