Good morning, students. This is a message from the school health office. Next Wednesday, all students in Grade 9 will have their annual health check-up. Please come to the sports hall, not the health office, at the time shown on your class timetable. The check-up includes a basic eye test and a measurement of your height and weight. It usually takes about fifteen minutes. If you wear glasses, please remember to bring them with you. Students who are feeling under the weather on the day should tell their form teacher, and a new appointment will be arranged. Finally, as flu is very contagious at this time of year, we strongly recommend washing your hands regularly and staying at home if you have a high temperature.
